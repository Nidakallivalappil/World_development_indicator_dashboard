# World Development Indicators (WDI) – Railway Infrastructure Analysis

## Project Overview

This project uses the **World Development Indicators (WDI)** dataset from the World Bank to analyze railway infrastructure and passenger travel across different countries.

The dashboard was developed using **Tableau Public** to compare railway length, passenger distance travelled, GDP per capita, ease of doing business, and international tourism.

The main objective is to identify patterns in railway infrastructure and passenger usage that can support better railway planning and development.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze railway infrastructure across different countries.
- Compare railway length and passenger distance travelled.
- Identify countries with high passenger travel.
- Compare railway infrastructure shares across countries.
- Analyze the relationship between GDP per capita and ease of doing business.
- Explore international tourism across selected countries.
- Provide insights to support data-driven railway improvement planning.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Tableau Public** – Dashboard creation and data visualization
- **World Bank WDI Dataset** – Data source
- **Calculated Fields** – Creating additional measures
- **Filters and Sets** – Comparing selected countries

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Railway Length vs. Passenger Distance Travelled

Compares railway infrastructure and passenger distance travelled across countries to understand differences in railway network size and passenger usage.

### 2. Passenger Travel Analysis

The dashboard shows that the top 10 selected countries account for **90.97%** of total passenger distance travelled, while the remaining countries account for **9.03%**.

### 3. Railway Infrastructure Analysis

The top 10 selected countries account for **60.05%** of total railway length, while the remaining countries account for **39.95%**.

### 4. Business Score vs. GDP per Capita

A scatter plot compares ease of doing business scores with GDP per capita across selected countries. This provides additional economic context for country comparisons.

### 5. Country Map Visualization

A map highlights selected countries and supports geographical comparison.

### 6. International Tourism Analysis

Compares incoming tourism figures for selected countries to explore tourism-related travel patterns.

## Key Findings

- Passenger distance travelled is concentrated among the selected top 10 countries.
- The selected top 10 countries account for a larger share of passenger distance travelled (90.97%) than railway length (60.05%).
- Railway network size and passenger usage do not have identical distributions across the selected countries.
- GDP per capita, business scores, and tourism indicators provide additional context for understanding differences between countries.

*Note: These findings reflect the current dashboard's country selection and calculations. They should be verified against the source data and reporting years before being used in formal reports.*

## How Can This Analysis Help Improve Railways?

1. **Infrastructure Planning:** Compare railway network length with passenger demand to identify areas for further investigation.
2. **Capacity Improvement:** Investigate high passenger demand alongside data on congestion, train frequency, and railway capacity.
3. **Better Resource Allocation:** Use railway condition, maintenance costs, and demand forecasts to evaluate investment priorities.
4. **Tourism Connectivity:** Explore whether major tourist destinations have suitable railway connections.
5. **Country Benchmarking:** Compare railway infrastructure and usage using consistent data years and measurement units.
6. **Data-Driven Decisions:** Combine these insights with railway safety, punctuality, accessibility, and service quality indicators.

These recommendations require additional railway-specific data before investment decisions can be made.

## Limitations

- Some countries may have missing data or different reporting years.
- Results depend on the selected countries and dashboard filters.
- GDP per capita and business scores do not directly measure railway efficiency.
- Railway length and passenger distance travelled alone cannot measure service quality, safety, affordability, or reliability.
- Further railway-specific data is required to make reliable investment recommendations.
